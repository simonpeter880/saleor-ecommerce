# Quick Guide: Add Your First Product (5 Minutes)

## 🎯 Let's Add a Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra!

### Step 1: Access Dashboard (30 seconds)
1. Open: **https://techhub.eu.saleor.cloud/dashboard/**
2. Log in with your Saleor Cloud credentials

---

### Step 2: Create Category (1 minute)
1. Click **Catalog** in left sidebar
2. Click **Categories**
3. Click **Create Category** button (top right)
4. Fill in:
   - **Name**: `Smartphones`
   - **Description**: `Latest smartphones and mobile devices`
5. Click **Save** (top right)

✅ Category created!

---

### Step 3: Create Product (3 minutes)
1. Click **Catalog** → **Products**
2. Click **Create Product** button (top right)

**Fill in the form**:

#### General Information
- **Name**: `Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra`
- **Description**: Copy-paste this:
```
🔥 Premium flagship smartphone with cutting-edge features!

✨ Key Features:
• 6.8" Dynamic AMOLED 2X display
• 200MP main camera with AI zoom
• Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor
• 12GB RAM / 256GB storage
• 5000mAh battery with fast charging
• IP68 water & dust resistance
• S Pen included

📦 What's in the box:
• Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra
• S Pen
• USB-C cable
• Ejection pin
• Quick start guide

🚚 Free delivery in Kampala!
🛡️ 1-year warranty included
```

#### Organization
- **Category**: Select `Smartphones` (you just created this)
- **Product Type**: Select `Default Type` (or create "Electronics")

#### Pricing (scroll down)
- **Price**: `4500000` (this is UGX 4,500,000)
- **Cost**: Leave blank (optional)

#### Inventory
- **SKU**: `SAMS24U-256-BLK`
- **Track inventory**: ✅ Toggle ON
- **Quantity**: `10`

#### Shipping
- **Weight**: `0.233` kg
- **Requires shipping**: ✅ Keep checked

#### Media (Important!)
1. Scroll to **Media** section
2. Click **Upload**
3. Upload a product image (or use placeholder for now)
   - You can find free images at: unsplash.com/s/photos/samsung-galaxy

#### Visibility
- Make sure **Published** is toggled ON
- Check **Available for purchase**

4. Click **Save** (top right)

✅ Product created!

---

### Step 4: View Your Product (30 seconds)

1. Go to your storefront: **http://localhost:3000/default-channel**
2. Click **Products** or **Shop Now**
3. You should see your Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra!

🎉 **SUCCESS!** Your first product is live!

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Product not showing?**
- Check it's **Published** (toggle in product editor)
- Check it's in **default-channel** (Manage Channels section)
- Check **Available for purchase** is ON
- Refresh your browser

**No images?**
- Upload at least 1 image in Media section
- JPG or PNG format
- Max 5MB per image

**Wrong price?**
- Price should be in UGX without commas
- Example: 4500000 (not 4,500,000)
- Storefront will format it nicely
